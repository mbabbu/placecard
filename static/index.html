Placecard

Seat every table with people who will actually talk.

Paste your guest list with each person's favourites. An AI agent reads everyone's taste through Qloo's cultural taste graph, seats the room so each table shares real common ground and nobody is stuck with only strangers, honours your requirements, and writes a conversation-starter card for every table.

SAMPLE DATA. No Qloo key is configured on this server, so favourites resolve against a small hand-written catalogue and taste vectors are computed from hand-written tags, not from Qloo. Everything else (the agent loop, the optimiser, the map) is the real thing.

How it works

Taste profiles from Qloo

Each guest's favourites are resolved with Qloo /search. One taste-analysis call per guest (/v2/insights with filter.type=urn:tag) returns the tags that characterise them: a tag-affinity vector. Names are never sent to Qloo.

An optimiser, not a guess

Pairwise compatibility is the cosine of idf-weighted tag vectors. Tables are filled to maximise the fit of the least-matched guest and the average table cohesion, with a swap local search, while your requirements (together, apart, fixed table, head table) stay hard constraints.

An agent in charge

An LLM (Claude or Gemini) calls seven tools: build_taste_profiles, apply_constraint, seat_guests, explain_table, table_card, ask_organizer, present_plan. It turns your wording into validated constraints and can ask you a question.

Cards you can trust

A table card may only name tags the table shares in Qloo and favourites its guests listed. The server rejects any card that mentions anything else. Affinities describe aggregate taste patterns, not facts about a person.
